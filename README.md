**Meta Ad Performance Dashboard (Power BI)**

**Overview**

This project focuses on analyzing advertising performance across Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram) using Power BI.
It combines ad event data, campaign budgets, ad details, and user demographics to track the complete marketing funnel from impressions to purchases. The dashboard helps in understanding campaign effectiveness, audience behavior, and conversion efficiency.

**What I Did**

Worked with 4 datasets:
  ad_events (user interactions)
  ads (ad type, platform, targeting)
  campaigns (budget and campaign details)
  users (demographics and location)

  
The dashboard includes the following sections:

KPI Overview
  - Impressions, Clicks, Shares, Engagements, Purchases
  - CTR, Engagement Rate, Conversion Rate
  - Budget metrics
      
Engagement by Gender (Donut Chart)
Engagement by Age Group (Bar Chart)
Geographic Analysis - Performance by Country
Time-Based Analysis
Weekly Trend by Ad Type
Hourly Trend of Engagement
Campaign Insights
Calendar heatmap for monthly activity

**Key Insights**

High CTR (~11%) and engagement rate (~13%) indicate strong ad creatives and targeting at the top of the funnel
Low purchase rate (~0.6%) shows weak conversion at the bottom of the funnel, indicating need for better landing pages or retargeting
Female users contribute the highest engagement, especially in the 18–30 age group
Countries like India, US, and Brazil contribute significantly to overall engagement, indicating strong audience reach in these regions.
Video and Stories ads perform better compared to Image and Carousel formats.
Engagement peaks during afternoon and evening hours, suggesting optimal ad scheduling windows.

**Conclusion**

This project demonstrates how marketing data can be transformed into actionable insights using Power BI.
While campaigns perform well in terms of reach and engagement, there is a clear gap in conversion efficiency. Improving targeting, ad formats, and post-click experience can significantly enhance ROI.

Tools Used- Power BI (Including Power Query & DAX)
